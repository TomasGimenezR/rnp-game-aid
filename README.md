# R&P! Game Aid
In R&P! Game Aid you get to fully host rooms for you and your players and play 'Rush & Punch!'. All the mechanics have been implemented to ease up online play, so you can focus on the fun.

## What it offers
* Create or join rooms without the need to log in or register. We don't want nor care about your personal information
* Make any of the Rolls this game has in its mechanics, and everyone in the room gets to see the results
* The application keeps track of Hope, Dread, Momentum and Drama for you
* Reset your Dice Pool when needed
* Keep track of who has already flipped their Round Coin and who hasn't

## What it does not offer
* Character sheets and/or Archetype abilities
* Entities
* Hero Paths and Missions
* Downtime Challenges
